iris_avg_fdr / iris_avg_f1: automatic patch-method metrics following IRIS's definitions. A flow counts as relevant when its source, sink or connecting TFG route matches a recorded fixed method; this is not an exploitability judgment. FDR = 1 - patch precision, with recall = detected per run. Scores are averaged over runs and then subjects; engine, path extraction and aggregation differences prevent direct comparison with published IRIS scores. These metrics need no flow labels. precision_mean and cves_confirmed are separate label-based estimates and counts. cves_tp_reported counts subjects where at least one run reports a TP-labelled flow, including flows outside the recorded patch methods. The study labels are provisional LLM judgments without human verification.

Sampling: `labels/sampling_manifest.json` freezes the original 10-per-config/union-GT draws. For a flow eligible in several independent draws, inclusion probability is one minus the product of its nonselection probabilities. Only originally selected current TP/FP judgements enter the weighted TP total. A covered domain containing no sampled flow has HT=0 and coverage=0; that estimate does not label unseen flows FP. A new/zero-probability reported key or a missing selected judgement makes the affected estimate unknown. `precision_per_run.csv` gives denominators, coverage and statuses; `precision_provenance.json` records hashes and assumptions. The old descriptive estimator is retained as `precision_descriptive_mean` only. These estimates remain conditional on the judgement quality; HT is not clipped and no confidence interval is claimed.
